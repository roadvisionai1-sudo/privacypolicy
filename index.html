Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ReRoad for Android (“ReRoad,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the app. ReRoad lets you photograph or choose a road image, request AI analysis and visual road-improvement concepts, save projects on your device, and optionally prepare a share card.

In brief: Photos you submit for AI processing are sent over the internet to our Firebase-hosted service and then to Google’s Gemini service. Project records and generated images are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server-side image library. If you choose to add GPS coordinates or a location description to a share card, that information is included in the content you share.

1. Information we handle

  • Road images: Photos captured with the camera or selected from your gallery. The app sends a resized and compressed image with a processing prompt when you request analysis, image generation, or project recommendations. The app can send an original image if it cannot decode it for resizing.
  • AI inputs and outputs: The submitted image, image format, prompts describing the requested road analysis or changes, and results such as detected road features, road type, notes, a generated image, benefits, and suggested municipal projects.
  • Local project information: Project identifiers, source and generated image file paths, road type, creation time, and saved benefits and project suggestions. These records are held in app preferences; image files are held in the app’s private storage. The app also stores its AI endpoint and model settings locally.
  • Anonymous app session and security data: The app creates or uses an anonymous Firebase Authentication account to obtain a session token for its AI service. It does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Firebase App Check provides an app-attestation token to protect the service.
  • Network and service usage data: Our proxy applies daily limits using a hash of the anonymous account identifier and a hash of the request IP address. It records per-endpoint daily counters and an expiry timestamp. The service also logs operational details such as endpoint, response status, duration, image format and byte count, AI model, token usage, and error information. Google Cloud infrastructure may process network addresses and request metadata to deliver and secure the service.
  • Optional share-card information: If you choose to add it, the app reads your current GPS coordinates after requesting location permission. You may also enter a location description and personal message. The app uses these, the road images, date, road-health score, and selected improvements to create a share card. This content is not included in the AI request; it is passed to Android’s sharing interface only when you tap Share.
  • Permissions: Camera access is used to take a road photo. Location access is used only when you explicitly choose the optional GPS action while preparing a share card. Android’s photo picker/gallery and storage mechanisms are used to choose or save images. The app does not request microphone access.

2. How information is used

We use the information above to provide road-image analysis and visualizations, generate project recommendations, keep your projects on your device, create a shareable image when requested, authenticate and attest app requests, enforce usage limits, diagnose failures, and protect the service from abuse. We do not use submitted road images to advertise to you.

3. When information is shared

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: The app uses Firebase Authentication for anonymous sessions and Firebase App Check for app attestation. Its backend is hosted as a Google Cloud/Firebase Cloud Function and uses Cloud Firestore for usage counters. Google infrastructure processes the service requests and related network/operational data.
  • Google Gemini: For each AI feature you invoke, the backend forwards the submitted image and task prompt to Google’s Gemini API and returns the response. Google processes that content to provide the requested AI output under Google’s applicable terms and privacy practices. Review the Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API additional terms. The app’s backend does not intentionally keep a permanent copy of submitted images or AI results, but processing by Google and infrastructure providers is governed by their terms and retention practices.
  • Android share targets you select: When you use Share, Android presents available apps or services. The recipient you choose receives the share card and accompanying text, which may contain images, a message, a location description, GPS coordinates, date, and road recommendations. Those recipients handle the content under their own policies. Sharing is optional and controlled by you.
  • Legal and safety disclosures: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the public, investigate abuse, or protect our rights and service.

We do not sell personal information. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in its current implementation. This may change only with an update to the app and this policy.

4. Storage and retention

Original photos, generated images, and saved project records are stored locally on your device. You can delete saved projects from within the app; associated image files or temporary files may require deletion through Android storage controls. Uninstalling the app generally removes its private app data, but does not remove images you separately saved to your gallery or content already shared with another service. Temporary resized image files may remain in the app’s temporary storage until Android clears them.

Our backend handles image and prompt content to complete a request and does not intentionally save those contents as project history. It stores usage counters with expiry timestamps intended to support short-lived rate limiting (the configured expiry is approximately two days after the UTC day ends). Actual deletion depends on the Firebase/Firestore expiry configuration. Cloud service logs may be retained according to the Google Cloud project’s logging settings; those settings are not defined by the Android app. We cannot erase copies already received by Google Gemini or a share recipient; their retention is governed by their policies and settings.

5. Location, image metadata, and sensitive content

ReRoad does not read location continuously or attach GPS coordinates to AI requests. GPS is obtained only after you press the optional location control on the share-card screen. If included, coordinates and any location text become visible to recipients of the share. Photos may depict people, vehicles, license plates, homes, or other identifying details. Do not submit content you do not have permission to use or disclose. Image resizing and JPEG recompression normally removes embedded metadata, but if an image cannot be decoded, it may be sent without that transformation; avoid relying on the app to remove all embedded metadata.

6. Your choices and rights

You choose whether to grant camera or location permission, submit an image, generate a share card, and select a share recipient. You can deny permissions in Android settings, remove local projects in the app, clear app data or uninstall it, and avoid submitting identifying images or adding location details. To request information about or deletion of backend account or usage data, contact us at ReRoadAI1@gmail.com and include enough information for us to investigate; never send passwords or authentication tokens.

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to complain to a regulator. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify it and may retain information where law permits or requires. This app does not currently provide an in-app account deletion control.

7. Children

ReRoad is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the service, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Security and international processing

Requests to the configured service use HTTPS, and the backend requires Firebase authentication and app attestation. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google and any share recipient may process information in countries outside your own, subject to their terms and applicable law.
