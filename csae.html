1. Our commitment and scope

RoadVision AI is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation and to acting on credible safety reports. These standards apply to everyone who uses RoadVision AI, including people who submit images, prompts, text, or other material for analysis or image generation, and anyone who receives content created or shared from the app.

RoadVision AI is a road-image analysis and visualization app. It is not designed for children under 13 and does not provide user profiles, social feeds, private messaging, random chat, or dating features. Users may submit photos from a camera or gallery, request AI processing, save projects on their device, and choose to share a generated card outside the app. These standards apply to all of those uses, including attempts to use road imagery, prompts, generated results, or share cards to sexualize or exploit a child.

For these standards, a child is anyone under 18. CSAE includes content or behavior that sexually abuses, exploits, or endangers a child. Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) includes visual depictions of a minor engaged in sexually explicit conduct, including real, altered, and computer-generated depictions. A claim that material is fictional, AI-generated, consensual, privately held, or intended as a joke does not make prohibited material acceptable.

2. Prohibited content and conduct

Users must not use RoadVision AI to create, request, upload, process, store, promote, solicit, distribute, or facilitate:

  • CSAM, or any sexualized depiction of a child, whether photographic, video, illustrated, edited, synthetic, or AI-generated.
  • Sexualization of a child, including nudity or sexualized posing, captions, prompts, scenarios, or transformations involving a child.
  • Grooming, including befriending, manipulating, coercing, or communicating with a child to enable sexual contact or obtain sexual images.
  • Sextortion, threats, blackmail, or coercion involving a child or actual or alleged intimate images of a child.
  • Sexual abuse, assault, inappropriate sexual interaction, or instructions that facilitate such conduct against a child.
  • Child sex trafficking, commercial sexual exploitation, offers, solicitation, or arrangements involving a child.
  • Requests for a child’s sexual images, attempts to identify or locate a child for sexual purposes, or instructions to evade child-safety protections or law enforcement.
  • Links, contact details, or other material that directs people to CSAM or child exploitation, or encourages, praises, or normalizes these acts.

Do not upload suspected CSAM to ask us to review it, and do not download, copy, forward, screenshot, or otherwise redistribute it. Send us a description of the concern and relevant non-explicit context instead. Do not include an image of suspected abuse in your report.

3. Prevention and product safeguards

RoadVision AI limits image processing to user-initiated road analysis and improvement tasks. Users preview a selected image before submitting it. AI outputs can be imperfect, and automated systems are not a substitute for human judgment or a guarantee that abuse will be detected. These standards apply regardless of whether content is produced by a user, an AI model, or another tool.

We do not operate a public content feed or an in-app messaging service. Project records and generated images are stored locally on the user’s device. Images submitted for AI processing are sent to our service and Google Gemini to fulfill the request; our service does not intentionally retain them as a content library. Users control any sharing through the Android share interface. Once content is sent to a third-party recipient or provider, that party’s systems and policies may also apply. See the RoadVision AI Privacy Policy.

4. Report a child-safety concern

Anyone may report suspected CSAE, CSAM, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or another child-safety concern involving RoadVision AI. In the app, open Settings and use the listed contact address. You may also email RoadVisionAI1@gmail.com with the subject “Urgent child safety report.” This contact route is available from the app’s Settings screen and does not require an account.

Please include, if known:

  • That the report concerns child safety, and whether there is an immediate risk.
  • A plain-language description of what happened and when.
  • Where the material or conduct appeared in connection with RoadVision AI, such as the feature used or a generated project, and any non-explicit identifier that can help us locate it.
  • Your safe contact address if you want us to follow up. You may report without providing your identity.

Do not attach, forward, or link to suspected CSAM. Do not put a child’s unnecessary personal details in the report. If the material is hosted outside RoadVision AI, report it to the service hosting it and to the appropriate child-protection or law-enforcement authority in your location.

If you are a child experiencing abuse or coercion: Tell a trusted adult and contact local emergency services or child-protection services if you are in danger. Do not meet or negotiate with someone threatening you. Preserve ordinary message details where safe, but do not download or resend sexual images.

5. Review, enforcement, and escalation

  1. Prioritize: We treat reports involving a child, suspected CSAM, grooming, sextortion, or trafficking as urgent and route them to the designated child-safety contact for prompt review.
  2. Assess safely: We review the report and relevant information available to us. We do not ask a reporter to send suspected CSAM. We may preserve limited relevant account, request, or report records when reasonably necessary for safety, investigation, or legal compliance.
  3. Restrict and remove: When we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM or other prohibited CSAE connected with a service or storage under our control, we take appropriate action promptly, which may include preventing further access or processing, removing or disabling the content, and restricting or terminating associated access. We do not promise a fixed resolution time because urgency, evidence, provider systems, and legal requirements vary.
  4. Protect evidence and privacy: We limit access to reports and related information to people who need it to investigate or meet legal obligations. We retain or disclose relevant information only as reasonably necessary and as permitted or required by law. We do not alert a suspected offender when doing so could increase risk or compromise a lawful investigation.
  5. Notify authorities where required: We comply with applicable child-safety reporting laws. Where required, we report confirmed CSAM to the appropriate regional authority, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States, and cooperate with valid law-enforcement requests. We may contact authorities when reasonably necessary to protect a child or prevent imminent harm.
  6. Enforce consistently: Depending on severity and available evidence, enforcement may include rejecting a request, restricting a feature or access, suspending or terminating use, and preserving relevant records for lawful review. Attempts to evade enforcement or retaliate against a reporter may result in additional restrictions.

We handle reports in good faith and may ask for clarification when safe and useful. We do not guarantee that every report will result in a particular enforcement outcome or that we can recover material after it has been shared with another service.

6. Legal compliance and cooperation

RoadVision AI will comply with applicable child-safety and reporting laws in the jurisdictions where it operates. Legal duties differ by location and may change. We will make legally required reports and respond to valid legal process through appropriate channels. Nothing in these standards limits a person’s ability to contact emergency services, child-protection authorities, or law enforcement directly.
