Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Welcome to RoadVision AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us when using our Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use RoadVision AI, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Camera Data & Video Feeds: RoadVision AI uses your device's camera to analyze road conditions, detect objects, or assist in navigation. Processing is performed locally on your device whenever possible.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we may collect precise or approximate geolocation data to provide location-based features, route mapping, and safety alerts.
  • Device Information: We collect information about your mobile device, including hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information about your interaction with the app, such as feature usage, crash logs, and performance diagnostics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the core features of RoadVision AI.
  • To process real-time computer vision analysis for driver assistance and safety warnings.
  • To analyze performance trends, fix bugs, and optimize application stability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.

3. Data Storage and On-Device Processing

RoadVision AI prioritizes user privacy by processing real-time camera video streams directly on your device. Video frames used for AI analysis are processed in memory and are not saved or transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly choose to upload feedback or crash reports.

4. App Permissions

To function properly, RoadVision AI may request the following permissions on your Android device:

  • Camera: Required for real-time visual recognition and AI analysis.
  • Location: Required for GPS-based features, tracking speeds, and contextual alerts.
  • Storage: Required to save app configuration files or user-initiated recordings/screenshots.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may utilize third-party services that collect information used to identify you. These may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

RoadVision AI is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
